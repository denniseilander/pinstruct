One focused workflow

Point. Instruct. Ship.

Step 1 · Point

Freeze what you see.

Hit the shortcut and Pinstruct freezes your screen into a still you can annotate — the active window, the full display, or any image you drop on the menu-bar icon.

⌃ ⇧ R
Active window Full display Import image

Step 2 · Instruct

Five kinds of feedback.

Every pin carries a category, so whoever receives the review knows instantly what kind of change you mean. Switch with ⌘1–5 without leaving the keyboard.

Step 3 · Ship

Hand off a brief, not a screenshot.

Copy an agent-ready brief, export Markdown with annotated images and per-pin crops, or post the whole review to a GitHub or Bitbucket pull request.
